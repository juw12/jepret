Camera sitting idle? Photos in, stock upload files out.

Jepret removes blurry and duplicate shots, writes titles and keywords, and makes the CSV files Adobe Stock and Shutterstock ask for.

  1. 1Pick the photos your camera sent to your phone, or the DCIM folder on your SD card
  2. 2Check the picks, type 3-5 words per scene
  3. 3Download the CSV, upload photos + CSV to the stock site

Sony camera to phone (Imaging Edge Mobile or Creators' App)? Set the transfer size to Original first. The default 2M copies are about 2 MP, below Adobe Stock's 4 MP minimum.

Other ways to earn with your camera: local paid jobs ↓

Honest note: AI images flooded stock sites and earnings per photo fell. Jepret does not promise passive income; it removes the boring part so testing stock costs you little time.

Get the app: Windows (Jepret.exe) · Phone: open this page in Chrome, then menu › Add to Home screen

Reading photos…

Recognises each scene on this device; photos are not uploaded. The first time it downloads about 90 MB, then it works offline. Check the words: the AI can be wrong.

or one file at a time:

Saved and shared photos carry their title and keywords inside the file, so Adobe Stock and Shutterstock fill them in when you upload.

Shutterstock: upload the shared or saved photos and the words are already filled in. Adobe Stock: upload the photos, then the CSV. Automatic upload from a PC: see tools/upload_stock.py in the README.
